fixed-feature-multiset assignment and explained it before execution. The method,
four conditions, ID-only context draw and resources were frozen before new model
calls. Existing independently trained classifiers were reused; there was no

## 11 November: frozen upstream and trained downstream

The owner proposed sequential cooperation, fixed the upstream and authorized
training only a downstream candidate-edge policy. After a saved-geometry
capacity check showed literal equal quotas were often impossible, the owner
selected the nearest feasible ratio while retaining each original total budget.
Codex implemented and registered a four-arm, three-seed TRAIN/VALID pilot before
new model calls. The downstream observes actual frozen target attachments
through a mean/count summary. It is a small conditional scorer, not a second
complete MonTi. The frozen historical score-source semantics were retained.

Two zero-real-data-query startup failures were preserved and charged to the
same CPU phase. A separately frozen compatibility amendment passed 34 synthetic
tests and four TRAIN gradient cases. The completed main run made 1500 downstream
updates; upstream and classifier parameters remained fixed. All final weights
and graphs were sealed before official-GAGA development evaluation. There were
no experimental API calls, GPU work or final TEST queries.

Separately authored AI audits recounted saved logits and serialized geometry;
they added no model inference and do not establish human verification or seal
chronology. The pilot passed its registered rule and improved over native MonTi,
but the small advantage over random candidate selection remains uncertain.
The complete staged scheme improved over native MonTi in this validation
pilot. The pilot alone did not isolate the value of receiving an upstream
action summary.
[Complete counts, controls and limits](results/downstream_cooperation/) retain
the negative seed and all controls. Human technical checks, actual student
contributions and final course authorization remain unverified.

## 13 November: continued author-checkpoint downstream development

The owner explicitly requested repeated evidence-guided downstream revisions,
with the author's released upstream checkpoint frozen. Codex implemented and
ran the three-repeat local-swap study, verified saved fits/logits/geometry,
and retained its null result. It then registered and executed a model-free
reachability diagnosis on all saved validation masks, with TRAIN-only block
feasibility inspection. A new whole-column recipient-learning study was frozen
and launched once, reusing upstream captures and matching exact donor rows,
activation and exchange counts in the random control. No new TEST/API/GPU
calls occurred in these development phases. Root-authored audits are not
independent implementation reviews or human verification. Publication summaries
were corrected to include the completed negative formal TEST outcome;
dated PDF/PPTX artifacts were not rebuilt or relabeled as current.

The whole-column study subsequently completed with no mean native gain and
a mean below matched random. Its original saved audit failed because an
empty serialized feature list had shape `(0,)` instead of `(0,32)`.
An explicitly separate audit-only source restored that shape and passed
13,538 checks while preserving all scientific bytes and failed receipts.
The root then froze a bounded direct-GCN proposal search to test prediction
error, with no new upstream generation or TEST access. Before any GAGA
evaluation in that follow-up, the owner clarified that the overall mean need
only exceed native and random; individual repeats/cases need not all improve.
Both the original strict screen and the new owner criterion are retained.

The owner then requested a larger strategy change. Codex stopped only its own
direct-search child, retaining 136 complete search records and zero official
GAGA evaluations. A contextual feature-residual architecture, independent
full-TRAIN GAGA teacher, exact feature-gradient preflight and six downstream
fits were completed. Both attention and mean networks declined against their
native and amplitude-matched random controls; the failed results are retained.

The next context-retrieval decoder initially misread CSC row-slice indices as
global columns. A first affine shortcut failed its preflight; physical graph
construction alone did not test the eligibility premise. Before any official
GAGA scoring, Codex stopped the invalid partial run at 197 cards and diagnosed
the new-code bug. An initial suspicion about cache mismatch was withdrawn:
all 890 current target identities have identical cached and actual two-hop
support. The original data/caches were not changed. A separate corrected run
uses CSR-normalized row indices, a CSR/CSC regression and direct cached/actual
neighborhood assertions. It completed 576 physical cards, three downstream
models, 2,672 updates and 960 official GAGA VALID calls. Both neural retrieval
and direct search passed the owner's mean-above-native/random screen; the
nominal random-contrast interval still spans zero. The root-authored saved
audit passed 19,593 checks, including 192 policy replays and no classifier
replays. These are AI construction/audit actions, not independent implementation
or human verification, novelty or completed course requirements. Under the
owner's prior conditional authorization, the next complete-cohort benchmark
keeps the existing models and controls frozen. Previously observed TEST
outcomes preclude calling it untouched confirmation.
