# Reconstructed One-Edge Attacks: Limited Endpoint Headroom

**Status:** validation-only diagnosis, not a selected method or demonstrated improvement. Coverage full evaluation remains **ON HOLD**. No training, GAGA/test queries, GPU use, or course approval is claimed. Earlier negative pilots and the inconclusive reproduction conclusion remain unchanged.

## Protocol, failed attempt, and audit

The [plan](../course/ONE_EDGE_DIAGNOSIS_PLAN.md) includes all 16 validation groups with one injected node and one allocated edge, totaling 81 target occurrences per attacker checkpoint. Three saved learned checkpoints produce 48 group/checkpoint cases. For each reconstructed attack, the probe holds its generated feature fixed and enumerates every legal target attachment endpoint: 243 endpoint configurations.

V1 stopped after 79 GCN forwards and 54.42 CPU-only seconds because rebuilding the selected endpoint exceeded the strict 1e-5 absolute-logit guard, despite identical predicted classes. Its [failure record](../evidence/one_edge_diagnosis_v1/failure.json), partial attempt, and source revision 8ecf14d are preserved; partial maxima are not completed oracle results.

A separate [pre-execution amendment](../course/ONE_EDGE_DIAGNOSIS_AMENDMENT_1.md) and [v2 protocol](../course/one_edge_diagnosis_protocol_v2.json), registered at source cbba66a, instead require exact feature equality, equal directed edge multisets, and strict predicted-class equality for selected-endpoint rebuilding, while recording numerical discrepancies. The nominal margin-change threshold remains 1e-5; it was not expanded.

V2 completed 355 GCN forwards in 230.69 CPU-only seconds, using zero GPU-hours. Including failed v1, the recorded total is 434 forwards. The [independent audit](../evidence/one_edge_diagnosis_audit_v2.json) passed a recount from retained logits and historical validation predictions without model inference. Adapter graph/feature-identity checks are source-reviewed and hashed, not independently regenerated by that auditor.

## Observed result: reconstruction is stable but historically discordant

Across diagnostic seeds 1729/2718, all 48 cases reproduce their generated feature, endpoint, and prediction vector. However, reconstructed predictions agree completely with the saved historical vector in only 17/48 cases; residual counts agree in 41/48. There are 55 target-prediction disagreements among 243 exposures. Accordingly, these are **reconstructed perturbations**, not identified original perturbations, even where predictions agree.

The largest identical-graph reattachment margin discrepancy is approximately 1.0204e-4. This is an empirical numerical reference, not an error bound. Count agreement alone is insufficient: different targets can exchange outcomes while the total remains constant. [Group-level evidence](../evidence/one_edge_diagnosis_v2/groups.csv) retains these distinctions.

## Observed result: seven extra wrong predictions under a restricted oracle

| Attacker checkpoint seed | Clean detector-correct | Reconstructed residuals | Best fixed-feature endpoint residuals | Net extra incorrect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 62/81 | 48 | 47 | 1 |
| 1 | 62/81 | 51 | 47 | 4 |
| 2 | 62/81 | 49 | 47 | 2 |
| Total seed-occurrences | 186/243 | 148 | 141 | 7 |

The oracle gains seven misclassified occurrences, or **2.8807 percentage points on this selected subset**. It uses observed validation outcomes to select endpoints optimistically; it is not a learned policy, a matched-query improvement comparison, a whole-validation gain, or a GAGA test result.

Seven cases have strict positive headroom, each with one gained incorrect prediction and no lost incorrect prediction. Their minimum pre-switch signed margin is approximately +0.8721, and maximum post-switch margin approximately -6.4258: these seven switches are well away from the zero decision margin and much larger than the observed reconstruction discrepancies. Separately, 22 zero-net cases contain gained-and-lost prediction swaps; those swaps must not be counted as seven additional net improvements. Every endpoint, including worse choices, is retained in [endpoints.csv](../evidence/one_edge_diagnosis_v2/endpoints.csv).

## Clean outcomes change the interpretation

Nineteen of the 81 target occurrences are already detector-incorrect on the clean graph. Repeated across three checkpoint passes, these contribute 57 pre-existing incorrect seed-occurrences. The reconstructed attacks' 95 incorrect occurrences comprise those 57 plus 38 new clean-correct-to-wrong flips; no clean-wrong-to-correct transitions occur. The restricted oracle yields 102 incorrect occurrences: 57 pre-existing plus 45 new flips.

Group 1285 is an important counterexample: it is already 0/4 detector-correct before injection. Zero residuals after attack therefore do not mean four newly induced flips.

In all 48 cases, the best residual count equals max(0, clean-correct count minus one). This is an observation for these fixed features, checkpoints, groups, and legal endpoints—not a universal one-edge capacity theorem. Aggregate residuals still total 141 under the oracle, compared with 148 under reconstructed selection.

## Offline attribution: where the available endpoint opportunity was spent

A subsequent [offline join of the same retained outputs](../evidence/one_edge_endpoint_attribution_v1.json) made no new model queries. All seven positive-headroom cases attached their only edge to a target that clean GCN already classified incorrectly; none selected a clean-correct target that resisted the fixed feature. Across all 48 reconstructed choices, 38 selected clean-correct targets and induced one new flip each. Ten selected clean-incorrect targets: three belong to the all-clean-incorrect group 1285, and seven had an available clean-correct alternative.

Across all 243 enumerated endpoint configurations, 186 attach to clean-correct targets and each induces exactly one new flip at that endpoint. The other 57 produce no new flip. No new class flip occurs away from the directly attached target in this matrix. This describes these reconstructed features and graphs; it does not prove that indirect influence or a different generated feature could never change another target. Clean GCN correctness also need not match GAGA correctness.

## Static source-role finding to instrument next

Read-only source tracing finds a concrete indexing concern. [MonTi.forward](../models/attack_model.py#L143) stacks target, candidate, then injected embeddings. Feature generation slices the injected slots, while edge generation receives the entire sequence. [edge_generation](../models/attack_model.py#L195) builds adjacency rows indexed relative to injected nodes, but uses those row indices directly in `e[src]` for the score. For one injected node, that score source is the contextualized first target slot. The [graph-building caller](../modules/experiment_handler.py#L397) correctly places the actual edge source at the newly injected node; this later offset does not change the earlier scoring representation.

This is a verified index-to-role mapping, not evidence of author intent, a proven cause of historical disagreement, or a tested correction. Transformer mixing can put injected-node context into a target slot. Numerical score ties and their consequences have not been measured here. Keep frozen scientific code intact and instrument score roles/order against the paper before proposing a change.

## Competing explanations and next controlled measurement

Endpoint choice has limited measured headroom here, while feature quality, clean behavior, fixed-graph structure, propagation, and budget constraints remain competing explanations. Holding one reconstructed feature fixed cannot rule out other generated features. Repeated groups and checkpoint passes are not independent examples.

The next priority is a separately recorded score-role and replay-concordance check: verify intended source embeddings against the paper, then measure raw score identities/ties and compare CPU with the original GPU path and graph/target ordering. Do not attribute historical disagreement to RNG or to the observed indexing concern without that measurement. Fixed structural metadata can further distinguish opportunity limits from feature weakness. Preserve budgets and report counterexamples before proposing a remedy.

The [complete summary](../evidence/one_edge_diagnosis_v2/summary.json) supports this narrower question. No improvement direction is selected by this diagnosis; any method proposal and full evaluation require subsequent evidence review and owner confirmation.
