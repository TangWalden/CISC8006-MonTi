# Supplementary research and experiment records

The main contribution is the staged division of upstream and downstream edge
roles described in the [current report](STAGED_REPORT.md). Its primary result
is the complete staged scheme versus the reproduced native MonTi baseline on
the registered validation cohort. The studies below address other component
questions or use other cohorts. Their individual outcomes remain available.

| Study | Recorded finding | Complete record |
|---|---|---|
| Staged scheme and matched controls | Mean GAGA MR 53.6486% native, 57.0344% random candidate connections, 58.1436% trained staged scheme. Primary gain +4.4950 pp with nominal paired 95% interval [0.5715, 8.4185] pp. | [All seed and case results](../results/downstream_cooperation/) |
| Direct action-summary mechanism control, 12 November | Removing the realized-attachment summary produced 58.7274% mean MR. The visible-minus-control comparison was -0.5838 pp with interval [-4.2838, 3.1163] pp. The original mechanism screen did not pass. This tests an optional information channel, not whether the complete staged scheme improves over native MonTi. | [Complete mechanism study](../results/downstream_blind/) |
| Fixed-graph feature assignment, 11 November | All four arms had 55.0576% mean MR and identical class predictions on the recorded 3213 occurrences. | [Method and retained results](../experiments/feature_assignment/) |
| Candidate-to-target edge reallocation | Primary mean gain +5.4790 pp on its separate cohort. The interval crossed zero and random target filling was slightly higher. | [Original method](LEGACY_REALLOCATION.md), [complete comparisons](../results/README.md) |
| Formal candidate-selector reproduction | Learned 43.7280%, random 40.1902%, paired +3.5378 pp with interval [-1.5415, 8.6171] pp. The frozen conclusion is inconclusive. | [Reproduction record](REPRODUCING.md#claim-and-paper-comparison) |
| Earlier proposals, diagnostics and technical pilots | Positive, negative, null and failed attempts are retained with their own scope and decision rules. | [Previous-study table](../results/previous_studies.csv), [archive index](ARCHIVE.md) |

The paired intervals use three seeds on fixed study cohorts. Target identities
and group occurrences overlap. Scores from different cohorts are not paired
comparisons. Random candidate selection in the staged study and random target
filling in the earlier reallocation study are different controls.

The original protocols, decision fields, numerical exports, failure receipts,
source identities and publication history remain unchanged. The current
editorial organization separates the main role-specialization question from
supplementary component analyses. It does not replace their registered results.
